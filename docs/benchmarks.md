## 7. Incremental runs over the synthetic history (`--incremental-entities N --incremental-days D`)

`materialize --incremental` on the history of section 6 (architecture §11.10): N dirty entities over the last D materialised days. *unchanged*: markers whose change left their rows as they were (a re-parse, a BSE mirror of a filing): each day recomputes the entities' rows through the run's slices and visibility memo and compares them with the stored rows; nothing is published. *split*: an accepted 1:2 split of each entity's security on the first of those days, so every day from it is published (G5, G4, write, cross-section of the whole day).

| Scenario | Dirty entities | Days | Days published | Status | Run s | s / day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| unchanged | 50 | 363 | 0 | DONE | 27.1 | 0.075 |
| split | 50 | 363 | 363 | DONE | 214.5 | 0.591 |

Section 7 and the incremental line of section 3 were refreshed on 2026-11-19 (QS2) by `python -m financials.bench --synthetic 3000 --legacy-days 1` (defaults `--incremental-entities 50 --incremental-days 363`); the other sections are from the run named in the header.

## 8. Full run on a copy of the live database (SP2, 2026-11-20, by hand; not generated by `financials.bench`)

`materialize --from 2025-07-22 --to 2025-10-16` (60 days, ~2,450 rows a day, results season) on a copy of the
2026-11-19 snapshot, 4 CPUs shared with two other test runs (load average 9-12, so absolute times are ~2x an idle
machine). Seconds per steady day (the run's first day, which builds every fundamentals block, ~28 s, excluded):

| Phase | Day by day (before) | Batched (after) |
|---|---|---|
| compute_rows | 6.54 | 3.33-3.37 |
| full-run slices (copy of the changing entities' rows) | - | 1.69 |
| write_day (daily rows + EVENT intervals) | 2.09 | 1.42-1.56 (whole publish) |
| breadth + three cross-sections | 0.27 | (in the publish) |
| G4 / G5 | 0.04 + ~0.4 (universe, gc) | 0.03 |
| commit | 0.13 | (in the publish) |
| **day** | **9.47** | **6.96** |

Extrapolated to the live 527 days on this loaded machine: ~83 min before, ~61 min after (on an idle machine roughly
40-45 and 28-32 min). compute_rows fell mostly from `_add_months` memoised (3.6 M calls in 10 days) and the
reverse-DCF bisection stopping at its fixed point; the remaining SQL is the batched readers of the entities whose
visibility moved and the Arrow inserts. `materialize --incremental` after QS2 on the same copy: an unchanged day
costs 0.18 s for 4 dirty entities and 0.37 s for 50 (59 days: 10.7 s and 22.1 s), so a daily run stays under 30 s
when the markers reach back no more than ~2-3 months; the markers' windows were the problem (architecture §11.11,
"Dirty windows"). Section 6 was not regenerated in this change.

Regression guards with generous margins live in `tests/test_benchmarks.py`.
